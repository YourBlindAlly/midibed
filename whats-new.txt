Grooves: a new "Extra drums in a groove" setting thins the hats, shaker, tambourine and toms (last level keeps only kick and snare). You can save your own grooves from the Drums page. Setup has a new section to load grooves, rhythms and journeys from a file (for example in Dropbox) and to save them to a file. Also the ambience lane and pack swells from the last build.
